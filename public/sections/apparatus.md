# apparatus

A fruit fly connectome, wired to a light bulb.

A glass bell jar with a fly, a copper wire, an incandescent bulb in a brass socket, and a meter, rendered in the browser. The bulb is the only light in the scene. Its brightness is the smoothed mean firing rate of a leaky integrate-and-fire simulation of 38,178 neurons from the Janelia FlyEM Male CNS connectome v1.0, running in a Web Worker.

The number in the corner is the current draw: spikes per second multiplied by the energy cost of one action potential (31.9 pJ).
